# Privacy Policy for "Hojaty" (حچتي)

**Last updated:** September 15, 2026

"Hojaty" ("حچتي", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application operates as a privacy-first, local Islamic utility app, how your device permissions are utilized, and our strict commitment to **zero data collection**.

---

## 1. Overview & Core Philosophy

"Hojaty" is designed to be a **local-first application**. Its main features include calculating prayer times, playing Athan alerts, and displaying Quran & Azkar so you can stay connected with your daily prayers.

**We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data.** All processing occurs locally on your Android device.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

### A. Personal Data
We do **NOT** collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, contact list, location history, or voice recordings.

### B. Device Permissions & Local Usage
To provide core features, the app requests specific Android device permissions. Below is how each permission is strictly used **locally on your device**:

1. **Notifications & Alarms (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `USE_EXACT_ALARM`, `WAKE_LOCK`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`)**
   * **Purpose:** Enables precise Athan alerts at exact prayer moments and restores prayer time alarm schedules automatically after your device reboots.
   * **Scope:** Local system scheduling only.

2. **Internet Access (`INTERNET`)**
   * **Purpose:** Used strictly for fetching updated prayer timetables, app configuration, or Firebase Cloud Messaging push notifications.
   * **Scope:** No personal identity data is attached or collected.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

All your app settings, prayer time preferences, selected reciters, and Azkar bookmarks are stored locally on your device using encrypted/local storage (e.g., Hive / SharedPreferences). 

* **No External Database:** We do not maintain any cloud database of user profiles or call logs.
* **Device Security:** Your data remains under your direct control on your physical device. Uninstalling the app completely removes all locally saved app data.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The app may use minimal third-party SDKs necessary for basic app functionality:
* **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):** Used solely to deliver push notifications (such as general announcements or prayer reminders). Firebase may collect basic device diagnostics (e.g., device model, OS version) as governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do **not** integrate third-party advertising SDKs or analytics trackers that profile user behavior.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

"Hojaty" does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is suitable for general audiences of all ages seeking Islamic prayer times and quiet prayer environments.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

---

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the app's privacy practices, please contact us at:
